# Language Lab — Product Steering

## What It Is

Language Lab is a free, voice-first practice tool for the U.S. naturalization interview.
It is styled as a 1970s language lab booth: individual study carrels, reel-to-reel warmth,
focused repetition.

## What It Is Not

- **Not a government service.** Language Lab has no affiliation with USCIS, DHS, or any
  U.S. government agency.
- **Not legal advice.** Nothing in this tool constitutes immigration legal advice. Users
  should consult a qualified immigration attorney for guidance on their actual case.
- **Not a prediction engine.** Practice results do not predict, guarantee, or reflect any
  official USCIS decision.

## Who It Is For

People preparing for the naturalization interview who want structured, low-stakes repetition
of the civics test, English reading and writing portions, and the N-400 biographical
question conversation.

## Core Experience

1. The user selects a fictional applicant profile (all profiles are invented).
2. They work through interview stages: intro, N-400 conversation, reading, writing, civics.
3. They receive immediate feedback and a practice report.
4. No real personal data is ever collected or required.

## Tone

Warm, encouraging, calm. Modeled on a patient ESL teacher, not a bureaucratic form.
Honest about limitations. Never alarmist. Never suggests what users should claim.

## Independent Study Tool

This is an independent study aid. Practice must teach users to answer truthfully from their
own circumstances. The tool never suggests answers that may not apply to the user.
